Who can see what

  • Public visitor Roads, imagery, elevation, and hillshade. No password.
  • Viewer The same public layers, after signing in.
  • Analyst Also parcels and installation polygons.
  • Editor Can add, update, and delete editor notes. Every other layer is read-only.

Parcel and installation maps are not on this page. Asking for them without the right sign-in is refused.
